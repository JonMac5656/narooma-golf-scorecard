Narooma Wind & Hole Strategy

Front 9: Coastal Cliff Tops

Exposed directly to Pacific Ocean winds. Crosswinds will exaggerate slice or hook curves. Play low punch shots into headwinds and favor the safe center-left sides away from ocean cliffs.

Back 9: Pine Alleys & Lakes

Ground wind drops significantly inside the tall pine corridor. Watch canopy top breezes that affect high loft wedge shots. Precision off the tee replaces wind calculation.

Hole 3 "Hogan's Hole" Strategy

Par 3 (141m Blue / 129m White). Oceanic chasm requires 1 extra club if onshore breeze is blowing. Aim for center-left of the green regardless of pin position.
